About Me

Hi! My name is Aaliyah Partida. I am a student at Northewest Vista at Alamo Colleges in San Antonio, Texas. I am currently pursuing my A.A.S in Digital Media. I have a passion for creativity and technology and I am always looking for a challenge. I can and will adapt to new situations and I am quick to catch on and overcome obstacles. In my free time, I like to play games, read books, and bake.

I first got into designing and creating when I was in Highschool when my mom was starting up her party and event decoration business. She knew I had always been a craftier person as well as had a good understanding on computer softwares so she started asking me for favors here and there. Slowly it turned into "Can you do this for me?" and then eventually I was doing a lot of the work for her. Both my parents are both creative people in their own rights and when they ask me to aid their whims, I hardly decline. The way I see it, I am helping them create something better for themselves. It brings me joy to help others achieve their own goals and I hope to continue to do so in the future. I am currently looking for a career in the field of Digital Media and I hope to one day be able to work for a company that allows me to use my creativity and skills to help others achieve their goals as well.
